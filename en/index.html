FP&A · Microsoft Fabric · DAX

Financial model for a global fintech

Problem: monthly close relied on an outside consultancy and on logic that was hard to maintain.

Solution: star-schema semantic model on a Fabric data warehouse, with P&L, EBITDA and variances in DAX using user-defined functions, plus root-cause work on source data quality, such as sign-inverted revenue.

Result: the team owns the solution, with knowledge transferred and rules validated by finance.
